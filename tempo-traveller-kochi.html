Tempo Traveller FAQs

Can I enquire about a Tempo Traveller with a driver in Kochi?

Yes. Share your pick-up point, dates, route and group size to request a driver-included trip quote. Vehicle availability must be confirmed for your dates.

How many people can travel?

Share your group size and luggage requirements so we can discuss a suitable Traveller and confirm passenger capacity.

How much does a Tempo Traveller trip cost?
Can we start at Kochi airport?

Include the airport pick-up, flight timing, group size and luggage in your enquiry. The pick-up arrangement and any waiting charges need to be confirmed.

Can we plan a multi-day Kerala trip?

Share your full itinerary, overnight stops and return date. Ask about daily limits, driver arrangements and any additional charges before accepting a quote.

How do I confirm a booking?

A WhatsApp enquiry does not confirm a vehicle. Check availability, written inclusions, total charges, payment terms and cancellation conditions with Drive On before booking.
